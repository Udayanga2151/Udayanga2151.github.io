Ink and Iron

My hands know both the pen and the plan,
Building futures no blueprint can span.
Logic and verse, together they stand,
Two languages spoken by one restless hand.

Written — 2025

The Weight of Wanting

I carry ambition like a second skin,
Heavy some days, light when I begin.
Not for applause, not to simply win —
But to prove the fire still burns within.

Written — 2025

Quiet Discipline

No one claps for the early hour kept,
No one sees the rest that I've stepped over.
Discipline is a promise, softly wept,
Between who I am and who I'm becoming, over and over.

Written — 2025

Alpha's Diary — Entry One

Some days are code, some days are verse,
Some days feel like a solved rehearse.
But growth was never meant to be terse
It's messy, human, and often worse before better.
